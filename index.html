My story

I was that tired guy

For years I worked on the tools as a roofer, a sparky and a butcher. Eight to ten hour days, and I was always tired and always chasing energy.

I tried the extremes. I did a full year of carnivore. It felt great at the start, and by the end I felt terrible. That's when I worked out that no single diet fixes it. You have to look at the whole picture.

Your energy problem is never just one thing.

So I spent four years studying holistic health under the Paul Chek system, and I'm now training as an Integrative Health Practitioner with Dr Stephen Cabral. Everything I teach is stuff I've tested on myself first.
