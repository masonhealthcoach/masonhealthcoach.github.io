My story

I was that tired guy

For years I worked on the tools as a roofer, a sparky and a butcher. Eight to ten hour days, always tired, always chasing energy.

I tried the extremes. A full year of carnivore felt great at the start, and terrible by the end. That's when I worked out no single diet fixes it. You have to look at the whole picture.

Your energy problem is never just one thing.

So I spent years studying holistic health under the Paul Chek system, and I'm now training as an Integrative Health Practitioner with Dr Stephen Cabral. Everything I teach, I've tested on myself first.
