Questions

Before you apply

Can we have a chat first?

Yeah, book a free 15-minute call. No pitch, no pressure. You tell me what's going on, I tell you straight if I can help. If I can't, I'll point you to who can.

Is this online?

Yes. Every call is a video call, so it works wherever you are in Australia.

Do I have to commit to three months?

No. It's month to month. Most people stay for all three phases because that's how long it takes to feel the difference properly, but you can stop any time.

Do I need to buy supplements?

No. Phase 1 is all habits. If supplements would help in Phase 2, I'll explain what and why, and you decide.

Is this medical treatment?

No. I'm a holistic lifestyle coach, not a doctor. I don't diagnose, treat or cure conditions or change medications. I surround the dragon: I work on everything around it, your food, sleep, movement, stress and mindset, so your body has the best chance to do its job. If anything medical comes up, I'll send you to your GP. Read the coaching agreement and health disclaimer.

What if I'm on the tools all day?

That's who I built this for. I did years as a roofer, a sparky and a butcher. Everything fits around early starts and long days.
